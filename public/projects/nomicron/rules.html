Setup

  • Start with an established Nomicron deck, or a new deck of blank cards and pens. Set them up like in the picture. There's a deck, a win condition, and active rules.
  • A deck can be any size you like, but we recommend 40.
  • If you're new to the game, shuffle in six of the intentionally terrible starter cards.

Gameplay loop

Draw five cards. On your turn, do ONE of the following:

  • Make a rule: Write a new rule on a blank card.
  • Play a rule: Place a rule card in the middle of the table, along with the other active rules.
  • Play a stolen card: Slam down a card from somewhere else. What counts as a card is is up to the players. It's now a permanent part of your deck.
  • ⚠️ If you lose your credit card this way, that's on you. Don't bother me.
  • Cheat: Do something illegal. If you're caught, you must write a punishment rule for yourself.

What is a rule?

A rule is a card that changes the way the game is played. It can be anything you like, but it must be followed by all players. If a rule is broken, the player that broke it must draw a card.

Everything counts as a rule. If your card says something like "trade cards with another player", that's a rule. It will just expire after what the card says happens.

If a rule contradicts another rule, rules are executed from left to right, with the leftmost rule being most important.

If a rule is removed from the active rules, it can be discarded into the deck or kept in a separate pile. Just make sure nobody can steal from it. If they still do, that's on you.

Rule challenges

  • Anyone can contest any rule at any time. The challenger and the person that played the rule duel by a manner the challenger chooses.
  • If the challenger loses the challenge, the rule stays in effect.
  • If the challenger wins the challenge, the rule is shuffled back into the deck.

The game ends when:

  • The first player has played their last card.

Permanency

At the end of the game, shuffle ALL of the cards back into the box. The next game will be played with the entire cursed deck.

How to keep it fun

  • Rule of fuckin bullshit: If a rule makes the game unfun, players can ignore it by collectively agreeing that its too stupid. If the rule is like, *really* stupid, the may elect the nuclear option.
  • The nuclear option: If a deck becomes unplayable, players may banish a card, removing it from the deck permanently. This decision must be made permanently.

A final note

If you think any of these rules are mandatory, you haven't understood the point of the game.
